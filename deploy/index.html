Your visit

Four steps from the first scan to the reveal.

  1. Scan

    First visit

    A 3D scan of your teeth and portrait photos of your face. No impressions, and nothing in your mouth for longer than a minute.

  2. Preview

    Same visit

    You see the planned smile on screen, then wear it as a temporary mock-up and look in the mirror before anything is touched.

  3. Craft

    Weeks 1 to 3

    Our ceramist layers every piece by hand, against your photos and your shade, in the lab one floor up.

  4. Reveal

    Week 3

    We fit, adjust and polish in one visit. You leave with a smile that looks like it was always yours.

Not if the work is planned around your face. You approve the shape on a mock-up in your own mouth before we make anything, and we choose a shade that suits your skin and eyes, not the brightest one.

Most treatments need only a local anaesthetic, and many need none. Preparation for veneers is minimal, often less than half a millimetre, so any sensitivity afterwards is mild and short.
